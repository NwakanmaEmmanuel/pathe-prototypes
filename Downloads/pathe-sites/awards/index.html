About the awards

Education grows because ordinary people refuse to stand by.

Behind every student who reaches university is someone who gave time, paid a fee, taught after hours or simply believed in them. These awards exist to say thank you in public.

Anyone can nominate. Teachers, mentors, philanthropists, parents, volunteers and young people are all eligible, as long as their contribution to education has been real and their own.
