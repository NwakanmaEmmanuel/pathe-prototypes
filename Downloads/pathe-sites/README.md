# PATHE Website Prototypes

Two clickable website prototypes for **PATHE Initiative** (Promoting Access to Higher Education), a youth-focused NGO that helps brilliant but less advantaged secondary school students gain admission into top universities and polytechnics, and succeed when they get there.

**Live site:** [pathe-prototype.vercel.app]

## The prototypes

### 1. Refreshed homepage
A redesign of the PATHE homepage that aims to build trust and make it easier to give, volunteer and get involved.

- Clear hero section with donate and volunteer buttons
- Animated impact numbers
- Programme tabs: Access, Support, Success
- Success stories carousel
- Donation widget with one-time and monthly options
- Get involved, news and FAQ sections

### 2. PATHE Education Awards
A gala-style awards website that honours people who have contributed to the growth of education in their own capacity, with public nominations.

- Countdown to the nominations deadline
- Six award categories
- Timeline from nomination to gala night
- Four-step nomination form with validation and a review screen
- Hall of fame, gala details, sponsors and FAQs

## Project structure

```
index.html        Front page linking to both prototypes
homepage/         The refreshed PATHE homepage
awards/           The PATHE Education Awards site
```

## Run it locally

```
npx serve
```

Then open the address it shows.

## Notes

These are design prototypes. The forms and donation buttons don't send anything yet, and text in `[square brackets]` is a placeholder for PATHE to confirm.

Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with help from AI (Claude) on the structure and first versions.
