Who we are

An educational access and mentorship programme, run by people who care.

PATHE mobilises recent graduates and NYSC Corpers to support less advantaged secondary school students in reaching the most competitive universities and polytechnics, and thriving once they are there.

See how we do it

Mission

To open the doors of higher education to every talented young African, regardless of background, by training leaders who teach and teaching students who lead.

Vision

An Africa where access to higher education is a right, not a privilege.
